Youth Protection & Safety

Stay Safe While Applying

Recruitment fraud preys on eager jobseekers. Keep these five principles at heart during every application.

1. Verify Official Sources

Always cross-check opportunities on the organisation's official company website or verified careers page.

2. Never Pay Money

Legitimate employers, learnerships, and bursaries never charge application fees, medical test fees, or placement bribes.

3. Guard Banking Details

Never provide credit card numbers, online banking passwords, or OTPs during an application process.

4. Inspect Email Addresses

Be cautious of recruiters using free webmail addresses (like @gmail.com or @yahoo.com) rather than corporate domains.

5. Report Suspicious Posts

If you encounter an opportunity that seems incorrect, expired, or deceptive, report it immediately to protect other youth.
